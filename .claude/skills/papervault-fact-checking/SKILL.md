# PaperVault fact checking

Treat every specific product statement as something to verify, not as marketing shorthand.

## Sources and checks

1. Check PaperVault feature and OS compatibility claims against the current App Store listing.
2. Check collection, processing, storage, and permission claims against `dist/privacy.html`.
3. For general technical or legal claims, use an authoritative, current primary source where available. Confirm that the cited page actually supports the sentence.
4. Separate what the app does from what iOS, iCloud, or another file provider does.
5. Check whether a workflow describes verified app behavior. If exact screens or steps are unknown, remove the invented detail or label the steps as a general checklist.

## Flag or remove

- Uncited statistics, invented tests, testimonials, awards, ratings, or customer outcomes.
- Guarantees about Google rankings, privacy beyond the policy, legal validity, recovery, or security.
- Claims that password protection makes every workflow safe, that compression cannot affect quality, or that stripping metadata removes information visibly present on a page.
- Expired dates, OS versions, pricing, or app details that were not rechecked.

Return a concise claim/source table with `supported`, `needs qualification`, or `unsupported`, then correct or remove unsupported claims. Do not silently leave a factual gap.
