# PaperVault content strategy

Plan content around the reader's task, not a keyword quota. PaperVault is an iOS PDF utility; its public product facts are in the App Store listing and the privacy policy. Read both before proposing product-led content.

## Workflow

1. Identify the searcher's specific job, experience level, and likely concern.
2. Group related jobs into a small cluster, such as scanning, organizing, signing, privacy, and OCR.
3. Search the existing `dist/blog/` pages and sitemap before proposing a URL. Improve or link to a useful existing guide instead of creating a near-duplicate.
4. Prioritize a topic when it answers a real user task, PaperVault has a verified relevant capability, and the guide can provide practical steps beyond a product pitch.
5. Give each article one distinct intent, a descriptive slug, and a small set of relevant internal links.
6. Avoid unsupported search-volume, ranking, conversion, or traffic projections.

## Output

For a topic plan, include the reader intent, working title, proposed slug, unique angle, verified PaperVault tie-in, source needs, and relevant existing guides. Clearly mark any claim that still needs evidence.
